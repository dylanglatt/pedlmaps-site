Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Pedl Maps (the "app") is a tool for finding bike share stations and planning bike trips. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is handled, and the choices you have. Pedl Maps has no user accounts and does not sell your data. Pedl Maps is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Lyft, Citi Bike, Citibank, or any bike share operator.

Information the app uses

Location and searches. With your permission, the app uses your device's precise location to show nearby stations, plan walking-and-riding routes, and provide turn-by-turn voice navigation. If you start voice navigation and allow it, the app may continue to access your location in the background (with the screen off) so it can keep giving directions until you end the trip. To calculate routes and find places, your location and the addresses you search for are sent to Google Maps Platform. They are not stored on any Pedl Maps server.

Saved places and preferences. Home, work, and other places you save, along with settings such as your unit and bike-type preferences, are stored locally on your device only. They are not transmitted to us.

Feedback you choose to send. If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, the category you pick, and your name and email if you choose to enter them. To help reproduce issues, the app also attaches the app version and build, the screen you were on, and your device platform and operating system version. Feedback is delivered to a private Discord channel that only the developer can read. Your name and email are remembered on your device so you don't have to retype them.

Diagnostics and crash data. If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Sentry. It includes the error, the app version, and your device model and operating system. Network requests are not recorded, and any web address in a report is stripped of its parameters, so reports do not include your location, searched addresses, or any keys. Crash reports are not linked to your identity.

Third-party services

To provide its features, the app shares the minimum data necessary with the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Maps Platform — routing, place search, and geocoding (receives your location and searches) (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — spoken directions. The text of each direction, which includes street names and the names of your stations and destination, is sent to generate audio (Google Privacy Policy).
  • OpenRouteService — alternative routing (OpenRouteService Privacy Policy).
  • Discord — delivery of feedback you choose to send (Discord Privacy Policy).
  • Bike share GBFS feed — public, real-time station availability data (no personal data is sent).
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics (Sentry Privacy Policy).

How your information is used

Information is used only to operate the app's features: showing nearby stations, calculating and narrating routes, remembering your saved places, responding to your feedback, and fixing crashes. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Data retention

Saved places and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Location data is used in real time and not retained by Pedl Maps. Feedback messages are kept until the issue they describe is resolved, then deleted. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. To have feedback you sent deleted, email hello@pedlmaps.com.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can revoke the app's location access at any time in your device's Settings. You can clear saved places within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally on your device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@pedlmaps.com.
